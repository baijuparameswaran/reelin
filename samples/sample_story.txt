*EXT. PLAYGROUND - DAY*

A chubby, adorable little boy toddles across the sunlit grassy lawn. Mid-stride, a ball comes bouncing toward him — his eyes lock onto it, following its path until it rolls to a stop nearby.

He waddles over, closing the distance.

Just as he's about to reach it, a beautiful young woman steps into frame and picks up the ball. We see her for the first time: warm eyes, a vivid smile.

The boy stops short, puzzled, looking up at her.

She smiles down at him, hands him the ball, and gives him an affectionate pat. His confusion dissolves into a happy grin.

END
